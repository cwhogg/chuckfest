## Key Dates & Deadlines

- **Lottery opens:** November (for trips ~6 months out)
- **Lottery fee:** $10 per application
- **If awarded:** $5/person additional fee
- **Award notification:** Monday 5pm PT after lottery closes
- **Must accept by:** Thursday 11:59pm or forfeit
- **2-week window:** 40% released 7 days ahead at 7am PT
- **Quota period:** April 24 - October 18, 2026
